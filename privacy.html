DATA & PRIVACY PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Effective Immediately

1. Privacy Philosophy: Local-First Processing

DataBridge was built on a foundational principle: your business documents, customer lists, financial spreadsheets, images, and audio/video files are yours alone. We believe the safest way to protect confidential information is never to collect or transmit it in the first place.

Factual Processing Model: When you clean, merge, format, or convert files in DataBridge, all computations take place 100% on your local machine using your computer's CPU and memory (zero cloud uploads). Your file contents, column names, cell values, and media streams are never uploaded to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party AI APIs.

2. Data Classification: Local App vs. Payment Data

To provide complete transparency, we clearly separate local desktop application data from subscription checkout data:

Data Category What is Collected Where it Resides
Document & File Contents Excel sheets, CSV rows, images, audio/video files, Word drafts. Strictly on your local computer only. Never transmitted over the internet.
Application Usage & Diagnostics We do NOT track document contents, keystrokes, or user file telemetry. Local log files (if enabled for debugging) remain on your disk.
Pro License Verification Cryptographic digital signature token (Ed25519) and masked email. Stored locally in user config directory to allow offline Pro operation.
Billing & Payment Data Payment method details, billing address, IP address, tax ID, buyer email. Processed directly by Paddle (Merchant of Record). DataBridge never sees or stores card numbers.

3. Payment Processing via Paddle

All commercial payment transactions for DataBridge Pro are processed exclusively by Paddle.com Inc. / Paddle Payments Limited ('Paddle') acting as Merchant of Record.

  • PCI-DSS Compliance: Paddle is certified PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant. Your payment card information is securely transmitted to Paddle's vault and is never handled by or stored on DataBridge servers.
  • Information We Receive: When you complete checkout, Paddle notifies our server via an authenticated webhook containing only the necessary transaction identifiers (e.g. buyer email, transaction ID, subscription status, and expiration date) required to issue your cryptographic license.
  • For complete details on how Paddle processes buyer information, please review the Paddle Privacy Policy.

4. Safe Output & File Protection

DataBridge incorporates a Safe Output design across all format engines. The software does not edit files in-place or overwrite source workbooks, documents, or media files. Instead, all cleaned or merged results are written into freshly generated output files, ensuring your original records remain intact and recoverable at all times.

5. Website Cookies & Tracking

Our official marketing website (hosted statically on GitHub Pages) uses no advertising cookies, analytics trackers, cross-site trackers, or third-party tracking pixels. The website stores only your chosen interface language preference locally in your browser's local storage (localStorage) for display continuity.

6. Data Subject Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Union / European Economic Area or California), you have rights regarding your personal billing data, including the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your subscription email records. Because file contents never leave your device, you have complete autonomous control over your files at all times. To exercise rights over your billing records, contact support@databridge.app.
