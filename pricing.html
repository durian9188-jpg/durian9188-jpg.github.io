Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, license activation, and cancellation.

How does the recurring billing work?

DataBridge Pro is billed monthly at US$ 12.99 (or local domestic equivalent ¥9.99/mo). Your subscription renews automatically on the same day each month until you cancel. All payments are processed securely by Paddle, our Merchant of Record.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime without penalty. Simply visit the Paddle Buyer Portal at checkout.paddle.com using the email you entered during checkout, or email support@databridge.app. Once canceled, your Pro access remains fully active until the end of the current billing cycle.

How do I activate Pro after purchase?

After completing checkout, open DataBridge desktop app, click 'Upgrade Pro' → 'Restore License', and enter the email address you used at checkout. Your cryptographic Pro license will be downloaded and verified immediately on your computer.

What is your refund policy?

We encourage all users to test the 3×3 free trial before purchasing. If you encounter unresolved technical issues, accidental duplicate charges, or statutory cancellation requests, you may request a refund via Paddle or by emailing support@databridge.app. See our full Refund Policy for details.
