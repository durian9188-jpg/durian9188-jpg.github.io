CUSTOMER SERVICE & BILLING

Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · Effective Immediately

1. Our Commitment & Try-Before-You-Buy Philosophy

At DataBridge, we want you to be fully confident in the software you use for your spreadsheets and file workflows. To ensure that DataBridge Pro meets your requirements before spending any money, the software includes a generous, built-in 3×3 free trial directly inside the desktop application.

Zero-Risk Testing: You can perform 3 independent successful exports for Smart Table Cleanup, 3 for Table Join, and 3 for Multi-Sheet Merge (up to 9 free Pro exports in total) without entering any payment method or credit card details. We strongly encourage all users to verify real-world file compatibility using these free trial exports prior to subscribing.

2. Merchant of Record & Legal Framework

All international commercial sales and subscription transactions for DataBridge Pro are conducted by Paddle.com Inc. / Paddle Payments Limited ('Paddle') as our authorized Merchant of Record. Paddle processes all customer payments, invoices, currency exchanges, and returns in accordance with applicable consumer protection regulations and Paddle's standard buyer terms.

Refund requests are evaluated fairly based on actual product performance, billing accuracy, and applicable legal consumer rights. We do not make deceptive or unverified claims of unconditional lifetime refunds.

3. Refund Eligibility & Qualifying Circumstances

Customers may request a refund under the following valid conditions:

  • Unresolved Technical Issues: If DataBridge Pro fails to function or consistently crashes on an officially supported operating system (macOS 12+ Apple Silicon or Windows 10/11 64-bit), and our technical team is unable to resolve or provide a workaround within a reasonable period.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Charges: If you were accidentally billed more than once for the same subscription period due to a payment processing glitch, the redundant transaction will be promptly refunded.
  • Statutory Consumer Rights (EU/UK/EEA): If you reside in the European Union, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with statutory right-of-withdrawal protections, you are entitled to cancel your initial subscription within 14 days of purchase in accordance with applicable statutory law.
  • Accidental Auto-Renewal: If your monthly recurring subscription renewed and you contact us within 48 to 72 hours of the charge without having used Pro exports during the new billing cycle, we will happily process a cancellation and refund for the renewed month.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

To prevent fraud and maintain fair service for all users, refunds cannot be granted in the following scenarios:

  • Requests submitted after extensive usage of Pro features throughout the billing cycle.
  • Incompatibility with unsupported operating systems (e.g. outdated Windows 7/8 or Linux, which are explicitly stated as unsupported).
  • Transactions involving fraudulent payment disputes or unauthorized license token resale/tampering.

5. How to Request a Refund

You can submit a refund request quickly through either of the following two channels:

Option A: Direct via Paddle Buyer Portal (Fastest)

1. Visit the Paddle Buyer Portal at checkout.paddle.com.
2. Enter the email address used during purchase to receive a secure login link.
3. Locate your DataBridge Pro transaction, view the receipt, and select 'Request a refund' or cancel subscription.
4. Paddle's billing team will review and process the request directly.

Option B: Via DataBridge Official Support

Send an email to support@databridge.app with the following details:
• Subject: Refund Request - [Your Order/Transaction ID]
• The email address provided during checkout
• The Paddle Transaction ID (e.g., txn_...) or Subscription ID (sub_...)
• A brief description of the technical issue or reason for the request
Our team reviews and responds to all inquiries within 24 to 48 business hours.

6. Refund Processing Time & Method

Once approved, refunds are credited back to the original payment method used at the time of purchase (credit card, debit card, Apple Pay, or PayPal). Standard processing time is typically 3 to 10 business days, depending on the clearing policies of your card issuer or banking institution.

When a refund is completed, the corresponding DataBridge Pro subscription will be marked as refunded in our licensing server, and the desktop client will return to the Free Core Edition. Your existing local files will never be deleted or altered.
